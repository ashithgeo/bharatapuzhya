About Bharathapuzha Watershed

Understanding the river basin through geospatial technology

The Bharathapuzha River, also known as Nila, is one of the important river systems of Kerala. The river basin plays a significant role in the environmental, agricultural, ecological and socio-economic characteristics of the region.


This WebGIS portal is designed to provide a geographical platform for exploring the Bharathapuzha watershed. It integrates interactive mapping and spatial information to support watershed studies, natural resource management, environmental monitoring and geographical analysis.
